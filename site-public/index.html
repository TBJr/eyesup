EyesUp

Learn to type with your eyes on the screen. A short diagnostic places you on a track, then an on-screen keyboard fades key by key as your fingers learn.

Private by design

  • No account and no cloud sync of what you type
  • Progress stays on your device; you can export or delete it anytime
  • Works offline after the first visit

Open the web app Keyboard test page

Desktop installers for Windows, macOS and Linux ship in a later release. Until then, use the web app on a computer with a physical keyboard.
