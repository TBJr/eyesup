Teaching illustration 02 · The reach

One finger moves, then comes straight home.

Shown on every intro card and at guide level L0. The other fingers never leave the home row, so the hand never loses its place.

1 Rest

Fingertips rest on A S D F and J K L ;. Thumbs hover over Space. Wrists float just above the desk.

Hands-ready gate
2 Reach to E

The next letter is E. Only the left middle finger moves: up and slightly left. The dashed ring marks where it came from.

3 Return to D

It comes straight back to D, so every reach starts from the same place. That fixed start is what the fingers learn.
