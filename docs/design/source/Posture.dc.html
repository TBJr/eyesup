Teaching illustration 04 · Posture

Sit so your eyes can stay on the screen.

  1. 1Eyes level with the top of the screen, or just above it, so the gaze rests on the text.
  2. 2Screen about an arm's length away.
  3. 3Shoulders relaxed, back supported by the chair.
  4. 4Elbows close to your sides, bent near 90°, keyboard at elbow height.
  5. 5Wrists level and floating while typing; rest them only in pauses.
  6. 6Feet flat on the floor or a footrest.
  7. Shown once in Orient and again on break cards after 20 minutes of typing.
